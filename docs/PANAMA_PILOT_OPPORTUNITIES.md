- Ciudad del Saber innovation hub (150+ companies incubated)
- Dollarized economy - attractive to international businesses

**Education & Language:**
- Bilingual education is highly valued
- 10+ international schools with expat families

**Community Infrastructure:**
- Active Facebook/social media expat groups (100,000+ total members)

**Strategic Advantages:**
